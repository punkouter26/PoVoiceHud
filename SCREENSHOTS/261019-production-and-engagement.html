Ratings, recap, You/Them, Wikipedia sources, tray

Debug build with --demo (sample cards, no audio), captured with PrintWindow. "Before" is commit 5ec6071; "after" is the working tree on 2026-10-19. The demo now runs with call audio on, so the speaker badge appears. Its focus card has a looked-up source and a 👍.

The HUD

Before

After

1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. Looked-up source. The claim was searched on Wikipedia and the model cited one of the fetched articles, so the link says (looked up). A URL the model wrote itself still says (cited by the model).
  2. Who said it. With call audio on, each card carries a You or Them badge. In Simple mode (microphone only) there is no badge.
  3. 👍 / 👎. Was the card right? The chosen one turns green or red, and pressing it again clears it. Ratings go into the session log, and the tally's tooltip shows the last 7 days (👍 24 👎 2 (92% right)).
  4. Put away. ✕ dismisses the card; the newest line in the history becomes the main card. Copy is unchanged.
  5. You / Them in the transcript. [Mic 04:15] became 04:15 You:, with your lines in the accent colour and the call's in the normal text colour.
  6. Recap. Writes a Markdown recap of the current conversation (summary, decisions, action items, open questions, then the fact checks and transcript), copies it, and shows a card that opens the file. Hotkey Ctrl+Alt+S.
