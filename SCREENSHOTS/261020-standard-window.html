  1. Title bar with the app icon and name, on Mica. Drag it to move, double-click to maximize, and use Win+Arrow to snap.
  2. Minimize, maximize and close. Minimize replaces the old "click the headline to collapse", and close quits (as before, so does the tray or Settings).
  3. The header keeps the mic, the latest verdict and ⚙, but the headline is plain text now: there's nothing left for it to open or close.
  4. The pages fill the window at any size, down to 420 × 320, and scroll inside it. The window remembers its size and position, and stays on top of other windows.
