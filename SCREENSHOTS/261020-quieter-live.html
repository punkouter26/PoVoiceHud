A quieter Live page

"Before" is the owner's screenshot of a live session (a speech played through the speakers), maximized. "After" is a Debug build with --demo, also maximized, captured with PrintWindow at 150 %.

Before

1 2 3
  1. One verdict for five sentences. Whisper hands over everything said between two pauses as one line.
  2. Amber everywhere. Opinions ("they don't love the art"), garbled speech ("Never grants on the party") and fragments all came back as Can't tell, with a block of colour each.
  3. Lines run the full width of the screen, and chatter looks as important as claims.

After

1 2 3 4
  1. Readable width: the lines stop at 900 px and centre in a wide window.
  2. Chatter is dimmed, so the claims stand out. Each sentence is its own line, and each claim gets its own verdict.
  3. Can't tell is a quiet note without a block of colour, and it no longer takes over the headline.
  4. The headline keeps the last real verdict, correct or incorrect.
